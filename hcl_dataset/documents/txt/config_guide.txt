NIMBUSDESK CONFIGURATION GUIDE
==============================
Version: 2.2
Date: 2024-11-05
Publisher: Brightwave Systems

CUSTOM FIELDS
-------------
Each workspace can have up to 20 custom ticket fields. Supported field types
are text, number, date and dropdown.

BUSINESS HOURS
--------------
Business hours are configured per team under Settings > Teams. The default is
Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 in the workspace time zone. SLA timers pause
outside business hours.

EMAIL TO TICKET
---------------
Each workspace receives a support address in the form
workspace-name@inbound.nimbusdesk.example. Emails sent to this address create
tickets automatically.

TRASH AND DELETION
------------------
Deleted tickets move to Trash. Deleted tickets are permanently removed after
30 days.

LANGUAGE
--------
The default interface language is English. Available languages are English,
Spanish, German and French.
