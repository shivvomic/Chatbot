MIGRATING FROM HELPPILOT TO NIMBUSDESK
======================================
Version: 3.0
Date: 2025-07-01
Publisher: Brightwave Systems

SCOPE
-----
These notes are for the migration team. The import tool accepts CSV files of
up to 50,000 rows per file. Retention settings follow the Data Retention
Policy.

FIELD MAPPING
-------------
The HelpPilot field requester maps to the NimbusDesk contact. The HelpPilot
status Solved maps to the NimbusDesk status Closed.

CUTOVER
-------
Plan the cutover for a weekend. Email forwarding must be switched to the
NimbusDesk inbound address before the final sync. The final sync takes about 4
hours per 100,000 tickets.

ROLLBACK
--------
A migration can be rolled back within 7 days of cutover. After 7 days, a
rollback requires approval from the engineering director.
